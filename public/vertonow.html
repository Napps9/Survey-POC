01 — The Problem

Decision makers are flying blind.

You know your work changes lives — but proving it is expensive, slow, or so complicated it never happens.

Funders and partners ask for evidence you don't have, and reporting steals time from delivery. Traditional research captures opinions, not lived experience.

Over $100B is spent annually on insights that are slow, biased and non-representative — and marginalised, hard-to-reach communities are structurally excluded from the process altogether. As more research leans on AI-generated synthetic respondents, the gap between what organisations think they know and what's actually true is widening.

The result: missed funding, weaker stories, and less confidence in the impact you're already creating.
